Pintu Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Compliance with Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act

At Pintu (demoCompany LLP), protecting your privacy is our highest commitment. We do NOT sell, rent, or monetize your personal information to third parties. We collect only what is strictly necessary to fulfill your orders.

01

Location Permissions & GPS Usage

Delivering accurately to your doorstep

We request precise GPS location access to provide our signature 10–15 minute delivery service.

  • Nearest Store Matching: Coordinates identify your active neighborhood dark store and confirm delivery availability.
  • Rider Navigation: Your confirmed delivery pin guides the delivery fleet directly to your house or apartment gate.
  • Live Order Tracking: While your order is active, location services provide real-time updates of rider proximity. We do not track your location when the app is closed.
02

Personal Information We Collect

Transparent data inventory

We collect only the following essential identifiers provided during your use:

  • Contact Identifiers: Mobile phone number (for OTP login and delivery coordination) and full name.
  • Saved Addresses: House/flat numbers, apartment names, and landmarks to save you time on repeat orders.
  • Order History: Past items and digital receipts to enable re-ordering and warranty/issue support.
  • Device Metadata: App version, OS version, and network type to troubleshoot technical glitches and deliver OTA updates.
03

Payment Data Security Guarantee

RBI and PCI-DSS compliance

Your financial security is paramount. Pintu NEVER stores credit/debit card numbers, CVV codes, or UPI PINs on our servers.

  • All payment processing occurs directly through RBI-authorized, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways (Razorpay, Cashfree).
  • All network communications between the app and servers use industry-grade SSL/TLS 256-bit encryption.
04

Communication & Notifications

Essential updates vs promotional

We respect your attention and minimize unnecessary alerts:

  • Transactional Alerts: Essential SMS/Push notifications for OTP verification, order accepted, dispatched, and delivered status.
  • Promotional Alerts: Occasional discounts and coupons. You can mute promotional notifications anytime in device settings.

Complete control over your data
